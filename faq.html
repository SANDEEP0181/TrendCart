Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about shopping, products, orders, tracking and support on TrendCart.

How can I place an order on TrendCart?

Browse the products, open a product page, choose an available size when required, add the product to your cart and continue through checkout.

How can I track my TrendCart order?

Use the Track Order page and enter the order details requested there to check the latest available order status.

What sizes are available?

Available sizes are shown on each product page. Clothing may include S, M, L, XL and XXL, while shoes use available numeric sizes.

How can I contact TrendCart support?

Open the Support page and submit a support ticket with your order and contact details so the support team can review the request.

Can I see product details before ordering?

Yes. Each product page provides the product name, category, price, image, description and available options before you add the item to your cart.

Where can I find TrendCart categories?

You can browse the Clothes and Shoes category pages from the main navigation or homepage.
